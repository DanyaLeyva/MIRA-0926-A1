# MIRA-0926-A1

MIRA (Motion Intelligence & Recognition Assistant) is a vision-based intelligent system designed to recognize, analyze, and interact with human movement using consumer camera hardware.

## Research Question

How accurately and reliably can a modular vision-based assistant recognize and analyze predefined human movements in real time using consumer camera hardware?

## Project Goals

MIRA will explore the use of computer vision and human pose estimation for real-time human movement understanding.

The project is planned to include:

- real-time webcam-based body tracking
- human pose estimation
- recognition of predefined movements
- basic biomechanical movement measurements
- visual movement feedback
- gesture-based interaction
- an interactive desktop interface
- session results and movement comparisons

Additional interaction features, such as voice commands and intelligent feedback, may be explored as development progresses.

## Research Focus

The project will investigate the reliability of vision-based human movement recognition and analysis under different real-world conditions.

Experiments may examine factors such as:

- camera distance
- camera angle
- lighting conditions
- different movements
- recognition accuracy
- processing and response time
